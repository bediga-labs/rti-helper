# RTI Helper · సమాచార హక్కు సహాయం

**Live:** https://bediga-labs.github.io/rti-helper/

Write a correct Right to Information (RTI) application or first appeal in 2 minutes, in Telugu or English, on a phone.

- Common Karimnagar offices: Municipal Corporation, Gram Panchayat, MPDO, Tahsildar, Panchayat Raj, R&B, drinking water, Collectorate
- Ready questions for road/drain works, drinking water, street lights, and "where is my application?"
- First-appeal letter when there's no reply in 30 days
- Print / save as PDF, copy, or share on WhatsApp
- **Nothing you type leaves your phone.** One HTML file, no server, no tracking.

A writing aid, not legal advice. Fee and payment rules can change; check with the office.

Made in Karimnagar by [Bediga Labs](https://github.com/bediga-labs): AI tools built for Karimnagar first.
