After you file · దరఖాస్తు తర్వాత

  • Fee ₹10 (BPL families: free, attach a copy of the card). Offices accept different modes (Indian Postal Order, demand draft, cash with receipt, court-fee stamp), so ask the office which one.
  • Submit in person (get a stamped acknowledgement copy) or by registered post (keep the receipt). Central government offices: rtionline.gov.in.
  • Reply due in 30 days (48 hours if it concerns someone's life or liberty).
  • No reply or a bad reply? File a first appeal within 30 days to the First Appellate Authority of the same office (use this page). If information was late, it must be given free.
  • Still nothing? Second appeal to the Telangana State Information Commission within 90 days of the first-appeal decision.

This is a writing aid, not legal advice. Fee and payment rules can change; check with the office.
